# Editorial state and continuation

This library is a substantial first draft, with 1,017 articles rather than 1,017 independently polished essays. At delivery, 37 articles have independent full prose and 980 use authored premises expanded with shared role analysis, cycle history, and regional background. All articles exceed 300 narrative words. The [machine-readable audit](editorial-audit.json) reports approximately 68% exact repeated paragraph words beyond their first appearance. This figure does not count near repetition or recurring argumentative habits, so it is a lower bound on stylistic repetition.

The work has fifty genuine research batches of four random draws each: two People articles and two History articles. Michael Powell appeared twice, producing 200 draws from 199 distinct articles. The later foundational, connective, and landscape articles synthesize existing batches; they are not additional random draws. Dates record historical coverage and must not be read as the lifespan of a named person.

The connected graph and absence of broken links establish navigability. They do not establish that every automatic cross-cycle connection carries a developed causal relationship. The independently written current histories supply some concrete exchanges, but many links in the structured draft remain invitations for further integration.

## Revision priorities

1. Preserve each article's specific premise, then replace its generic role paragraph with a scene, material description, testimony, or consequential decision particular to that subject.
2. Move repeated background into linked regional and cycle pages where practical. Replace the removed words with subject-specific history so the requested article depth remains real rather than padded.
3. Diversify motives. Curiosity, affection, embarrassment, rivalry, pleasure, fear, grief, and theological conviction should complicate the repeated conflict between patrons and maintainers.
4. Make cross-cycle links accountable: identify an object transferred, a household migrated, a technique borrowed, or a change that harms or helps another region. Delete a connection that remains merely adjacent in the index.
5. Give each period distinct practices without treating cultures as steps on a single ladder. Retain minority interpretations and ordinary inhabitants who do not share their region's dominant account.

## Canon to preserve

An undertaking needs water contact, credible witnessing, finite scope, and a continuing material observance. A dry text alone creates no binding promise. The dead possess acquired claims rather than moral omniscience. Manifestations remain local and costly; they cannot produce unlimited food, energy, labor, or an obedient dead army. A ruler's ancestry does not grant control over every grave or spring.

Before altering a historical event, inspect its related articles and backlinks. Keep witnessed facts separate from claims by patrons, custodians, or interpreters. A supernatural effect can be verified while its cause stays disputed. Present year 1248 leaves room for repair, refusal, and departure; uncertainty need not lead inevitably to apocalypse.

Edit the saved dossiers for structured articles and the cycle JSON for independent articles, then regenerate with the command in the [project guide](../README.md). Recheck the audit after substantive revisions. Additional quantity alone is not the next meaningful measure of improvement.
