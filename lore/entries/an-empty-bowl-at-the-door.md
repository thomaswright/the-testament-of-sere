# An Empty Bowl at the Door

*Current expedition · Keth · Historical coverage: 1248 AFR*

The expedition to the Door That Opens for No One carries an empty bowl, spare ropes, and two copies of a plan whose most dramatic line is crossed out. A patron originally commissioned a descent and the appointment of a substitute claimant, hoping Last Season would make the visible chamber accessible. The custodians refused. They have no credible evidence identifying the original household, the promised act, or the intended recipient. A useful practice for known undertakings cannot honestly be expanded into a key for every unresolved place.

Liwen, the bell diver, advised the party about finite air and declined to guarantee another descent. The plan now permits only surface measurements and one carefully witnessed crossing if conditions remain safe. The empty bowl measures water lost from the plaster cistern afterward. Its amount will be supplied by the patron in addition to ordinary crew provisions. This requirement has reduced his enthusiasm but not yet withdrawn his funding.

A catalog house holds older inspection accounts whose full approach directions are withheld. Curious visitors have injured themselves on an unstable lower stair, and a collector tried removing a lintel piece without authorization. The Withheld Location Clause allows the party to inspect evidence through paired custodians without publishing every access detail. It protects an actual site and nearby households, not a profitable monopoly on mystery.

One young rope worker knows the Mother of the Kept Breath through coastal lessons. He brought its teaching bowl and was initially assigned the role of Ilem’s symbolic child during the trial. He refused, invoking the Learner’s Refusal Right: learning a tradition did not oblige him to enact a dangerous interpretation. The party removed the role and kept him as a paid worker whose practical skill they still need.

In 1248 the expedition has measured the roof and recovered no treasure. A dry wooden marker placed through the threshold remains visible beyond it, while every person crossing returns through the ordinary loop. Its inaccessible presence is unsettling enough without invented gods. The party will leave before stores run short, publish bounded observations, and record which questions remain unanswered. The empty bowl returns with a measured loss, a small fact the patron may find less satisfying than revelation and the custodians considerably more useful.
