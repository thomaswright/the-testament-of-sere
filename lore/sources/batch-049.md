# Inspiration batch 049: The Blue Wards After Victory

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Shuji Nakamura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuji_Nakamura)

Category: People.

A difficult breakthrough producing vivid blue and contested craft credit becomes kiln glaze; semiconductors and electric lighting are excluded.

## [Tomyris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomyris)

Category: People.

## [Soviet famine of 1946–1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_famine_of_1946%E2%80%931947)

Category: History.

Category: History.

Care institutions developing across different traditions become practical wards drawing on shrines, military nursing, and neighborhood provision.
