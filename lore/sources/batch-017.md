# Inspiration batch 017: The Rock of Borrowed Refuge

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)

Category: People.

An actor portraying psychologically complex opposed characters becomes a performer whose double role complicates a settlement’s public story.

## [Kemal Reis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemal_Reis)

Category: People.

A privateer-admiral mentoring a cartographic relative becomes a rescue captain and apprentice mapping overlooked shelters.

## [History of Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Gibraltar)

Category: History.

A strategically significant fortified rock with layered names becomes the strait’s contested hollow landmark.

## [History of Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Liberia)

Category: History.

A settlement founded by formerly constrained emigrants and its relationship with existing inhabitants become rescue followed by unequal civic membership.
