# Inspiration batch 035: The Secretary at the Restored Spring

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [John IV of Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_IV_of_Portugal)

Category: People.

Restoration of a dynasty after a long shared crown becomes island separation with continuing dependencies.

## [Eumenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eumenes)

Category: People.

A royal secretary becoming a commander while lacking the dominant group’s inherited standing becomes a contested outsider’s military authority.

## [Bay of Pigs Invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Pigs_Invasion)

Category: History.

A patron-backed exile landing failing against defenders becomes a return attempt based on mistaken expectations of support.

## [Battle of Ain Jalut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ain_Jalut)

Category: History.

A halted advance using tactical withdrawal near a spring becomes a bounded battlefield reversal, not proof of inevitable civilizational destiny.
