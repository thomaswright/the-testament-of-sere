# Inspiration batch 034: The Fourteen Courtesies at the Frontier

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [William Congreve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Congreve)

Category: People.

Polished comedy of manners becomes social negotiation staged through wit rather than borrowed quotations.

## [Ferdinand VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_VI)

Category: People.

A ruler praised for peace alongside a mass confinement order becomes incompatible public evaluations grounded in different affected households.

## [Ghassanids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghassanids)

Category: History.

A client frontier confederation with distinct religious practice becomes defense cooperation without uniform household rites.

## [Fourteen Points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourteen_Points)

Category: History.

Ideal peace principles and allied skepticism become fourteen courtesies tested against actual willingness to provide resources.
