# Inspiration batch 022: The Capital Carried Downriver

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Sally Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Field)

Category: People.

A performer’s roles as an organizer and a struggling mother become public drama connecting household survival with collective bargaining.

## [al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/al-Mu%27izz_li-Din_Allah)

Category: People.

A dynasty relocating power and founding a victorious capital becomes court migration and an unfinished ceremonial city.

## [History of Lesotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Lesotho)

Category: History.

A refuge polity forming amid displacement and negotiated outside protection becomes a highland coalition with contested boundaries.

## [Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroyalty_of_the_R%C3%ADo_de_la_Plata)

Category: History.

A late imperial jurisdiction reorganized around trade and security becomes a governor’s attempt to consolidate distant landing dependencies.
