# Inspiration batch 013: Spring in the Salt House

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Douglass North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglass_North)

Category: People.

Institutions shaping incentives and the costs of enforcing agreements become an exchange court with measurable benefits and exclusions.

## [Fei Mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fei_Mu)

Category: People.

A restrained household drama in a damaged small town becomes a play about loyalty, desire, illness, and ordinary maintenance.

## [Toltec Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toltec_Empire)

Category: History.

Contested traditions concerning an artisan polity become uncertain descent from a legendary craft capital.

## [History of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Chile)

Category: History.

Successive export dependence and restricted political participation become a preserving-salt town whose prosperity and vulnerability are linked.
