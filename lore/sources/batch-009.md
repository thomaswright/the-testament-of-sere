# Inspiration batch 009: The Days on Which Blades Sleep

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Helen Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Hunt)

Category: People.

Performance centered on ordinary household relationships becomes a comedy that makes civic cooperation comprehensible.

## [Larry Summers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Summers)

Category: People.

Public financial authority and contested risk allocation become a guarantee pool bounded by real grain and repair labor, not modern banking machinery.

## [Peace and Truce of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_and_Truce_of_God)

Category: History.

A movement protecting people and resources while limiting violent days becomes a negotiated agricultural truce.

## [Historical figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_figure)

Category: History.

Debate over significant individuals and incomplete evidence becomes the fabrication and correction of exemplary biographies.
