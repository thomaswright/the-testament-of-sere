# Inspiration batch 016: The Thread Houses After the Throne

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Richard Arkwright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Arkwright)

Category: People.

Coordination of invention, purchasing, and labor becomes a disputed handcraft network; mechanized factories and industrial mass production are explicitly excluded.

## [Menachem Mendel Schneerson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menachem_Mendel_Schneerson)

Category: People.

A teacher’s extensive educational and social network becomes distributed service houses with contested succession expectations.

## [History of the Assyrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Assyrians)

Category: History.

Cultural continuity across imperial and post-imperial periods becomes displaced weaving communities preserving changing traditions.

## [Muromachi period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muromachi_period)

Category: History.

Rival courts and patron-supported cultural developments become competing shelf authorities that cannot fully control the crafts they sponsor.
