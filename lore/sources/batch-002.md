# Inspiration batch 002: The Crown of Peeled Stone

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Jean-Bédel Bokassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-B%C3%A9del_Bokassa)

Category: People.

An extravagant self-coronation and later rehabilitation inform a ruler whose public spectacle conceals borrowed wealth.

## [Andre Geim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Geim)

Category: People.

Playful experimentation leading to consequential material discovery becomes hand-peeling a naturally layered stone; no modern physics apparatus is imported.

## [Vilcabamba, Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilcabamba%2C_Peru)

Category: History.

A last refuge confused with a more impressive ruin becomes a dispute over which abandoned site actually sheltered the defeated.

## [2014 Russian annexation of Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Russian_annexation_of_Crimea)

Category: History.

An annexation presented as popular consent becomes a controlled terrace assembly whose exclusions survive in material evidence.
