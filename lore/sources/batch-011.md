# Inspiration batch 011: The King Who Wrestled His Own Herald

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Mentuhotep II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentuhotep_II)

Category: People.

Reunification after a period of competing dynasties becomes a coastal king whose changed title announces a political achievement.

## [Vince McMahon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_McMahon)

Category: People.

Consolidation of regional wrestling promotion and a promoter’s performed villainy become royal exhibitions and control of local fairs.

## [Malayan campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayan_campaign)

Category: History.

Rapid movement along difficult terrain and ineffective bridge demolition become handcart raiders using paths their opponents failed to understand.

## [Anglo-Persian Oil Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Persian_Oil_Company)

Category: History.

An exclusive resource concession and later struggle over control become resin gathering; fossil fuels and industrial extraction are excluded.
