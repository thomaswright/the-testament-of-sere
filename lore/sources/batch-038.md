# Inspiration batch 038: The Wagons Beneath the Borrowed Capital

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Prokop the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prokop_the_Great)

Category: People.

A priest continuing religious service while commanding radical forces becomes a wagon general with overlapping roles.

## [Gianni Agnelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianni_Agnelli)

Category: People.

Concentrated dynastic craft wealth and influential eccentric fashion become a cart-building household and copied dress customs; industrial manufacture is excluded.

## [Beiyang government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beiyang_government)

Category: History.

Military factions retaining external recognition, loans, and customs access become rival capital offices whose formal legitimacy exceeds actual control.

## [Mossi Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossi_Kingdoms)

Category: History.

Long-lived kingdoms formed through warrior migration and intermarriage become a pasture confederation adapting beneath successive outside administrations.
