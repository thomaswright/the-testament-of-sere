# Inspiration batch 018: The Tour of the Fragrant Ruins

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Antoine-Jean Gros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine-Jean_Gros)

Category: People.

A history painter gaining patronage through a military leader becomes a tour artist whose images support political authority.

## [Roy Orbison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Orbison)

Category: People.

A restrained performer expressing vulnerability becomes a still singer whose lament interrupts expectations of public strength.

## [Deng Xiaoping's southern tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deng_Xiaoping%27s_southern_tour)

Category: History.

A retired leader reviving reforms through a regional tour becomes influence exercised outside formal office.

## [Spice trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice_trade)

Category: History.

Long-distance spice exchange and tales concealing sources become fragrant-bark trade through human-powered maritime routes.
