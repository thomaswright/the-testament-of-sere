# Inspiration batch 020: The Judges Who Walk Between Gardens

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [Valentín Gómez Farías](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valent%C3%ADn_G%C3%B3mez_Far%C3%ADas)

Category: People.

A physician-reformer confronting military and religious privileges becomes an attempt to unite practical civic jurisdiction.

## [Tinariwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinariwen)

Category: People.

Exiled musicians blending traditions and carrying political memory become a traveling valley ensemble using pre-industrial instruments.

## [History of Timor-Leste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Timor-Leste)

Category: History.

Partition, occupation, a independence vote, and destructive withdrawal become the eastern gardens’ interrupted self-government.

## [Frisian freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frisian_freedom)

Category: History.

Rural communal autonomy and elected adjudicators become walking judges without a hereditary sovereign.
