# Inspiration batch 025: The Saint of the Locked Wards

These four articles were drawn through the requested Toolforge categories. The lore transforms selected mechanisms; it does not reproduce the historical societies or claim historical equivalence.

## [A. P. Elkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._P._Elkin)

Category: People.

An anthropologist’s documentation combined with assimilationist policy becomes a scholar preserving practices while restricting their custodians’ authority.

## [Jimmy Savile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Savile)

Category: People.

A charitable celebrity protected despite ignored abuse complaints becomes a benefactor whose reputation shields access to vulnerable people; no graphic detail or real victim identity is borrowed.

## [Operation Torch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Torch)

Category: History.

A multi-landing invasion encountering mixed loyalties becomes a coastal coalition whose supplies and negotiations require scrutiny.

## [Bofors scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bofors_scandal)

Category: History.

Procurement kickbacks and diverted development funds become defensive-engine contracts funded at the expense of care ward repairs; modern artillery is excluded.
