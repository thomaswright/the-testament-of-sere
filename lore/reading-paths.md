# Reading paths

The encyclopedia contains a broad structured draft and a smaller set of fuller prose anchors. Begin along one route, then follow the connections and backlinks. Shared historical and regional paragraphs recur in the structured articles; their central premises are individually authored. The [editorial audit](editorial-audit.json) measures exact repetition rather than presenting total rendered words as unique prose.

## Enter the world

Read [The First Reckoning](entries/the-first-reckoning.md), [Dry Copies and Wet Witnesses](entries/dry-copies-and-wet-witnesses.md), and [The Doubly Held](entries/the-doubly-held.md). They establish the common calendar, what can actually bind an undertaking, and a bodily cost of incompatible claims. The [world bible](world-bible.md) states the limits that later stories must respect.

## A complete early story cycle

Begin with [The Leather Winter](entries/the-leather-winter.md), then [The Green Tithe](entries/the-green-tithe.md), [The Unannounced War](entries/the-unannounced-war.md), and [The Third Bowl](entries/the-third-bowl.md). Continue through [The Daughters’ Petition](entries/the-daughters-petition.md) and [Vesh of the Empty Register](entries/vesh-of-the-empty-register.md). Rescue, extraction, reconciliation, violence, and documentary absence accumulate across centuries instead of ending at a battle.

## The uncanny basin

Read [The Mother of the Kept Breath](entries/the-mother-of-the-kept-breath.md), [The Door That Opens for No One](entries/the-door-that-opens-for-no-one.md), [The Basin with Five Levels](entries/b019-the-basin-with-five-levels.md), and [Liwen of the Finite Bell](entries/b042-liwen-of-the-finite-bell.md). Finish with [An Empty Bowl at the Door](entries/an-empty-bowl-at-the-door.md). Verified limits and unresolved origins coexist; no discovery produces unlimited energy, labor, or authority.

## Living connections in 1248

[The Blue Tile Convoy](entries/the-blue-tile-convoy.md) links care, craft, pasture food, and northern depots. [The Last Season at the Peace Garden](entries/the-last-season-at-the-peace-garden.md) joins talks, ferry access, repairs, and famine provision. [Nine Voices at the Southern Gate](entries/nine-voices-at-the-southern-gate.md) connects theater, historical layers, kin care, and charter consent. [The Orchard Between Two Deeds](entries/the-orchard-between-two-deeds.md) makes the cost of incompatible valid claims concrete.

## Places to enter cautiously

[The Terraces of the Second Foot](entries/the-terraces-of-the-second-foot.md) turns imperial succession into a dangerous burial landscape. [The Cargo That Arrived as a Forest](entries/the-cargo-that-arrived-as-a-forest.md) connects source concealment with a warehouse disturbance. [The Plateau of Open Mouths](entries/the-plateau-of-open-mouths.md) preserves a battlefield whose breath resists its victory story. [The Stair Built for a Drowned Court](entries/the-stair-built-for-a-drowned-court.md) describes what an explorer actually saw beneath the cistern, leaving the ruin’s extent unresolved.

## Knowledge and everyday life

Visit [The Strained Culture Yard](entries/b008-the-strained-culture-yard.md), [The Color Without Rank](entries/b007-the-color-without-rank.md), [The Stone Families Tray](entries/b043-the-stone-families-tray.md), and [The Sweet Town Cooling Yard](entries/b046-the-sweet-town-cooling-yard.md). These provide useful beginnings in food, observation, classification, and handcraft pleasure. Their history includes failure, credit, and maintenance rather than a simple march from ignorance to industrial modernity.
